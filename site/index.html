~

There's no place like ~

In one command, take a fresh Mac and make it just how Matthew likes it.

Options

--minimal
Dotfiles only. Skips packages, plugins, and scripts.
--no-apps
Skips Homebrew packages.
--no-plugins
Skips Claude Code plugins.
--dir PATH
Clones somewhere other than ~/dotfiles.
--dry-run
Clones, then previews install.sh without changing anything.
--yes
Answers yes to every install.sh prompt.

Pass them after -s --, like curl init.bostonaholic.dev | bash -s -- --minimal
